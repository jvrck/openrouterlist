How these pairs are chosen

From current, paid text-output models with safe IDs and numeric prices, we select the 10 providers with the most eligible models, take each provider’s newest three, and choose the highest 3:1 blended-price model as its flagship. The meta and meta-llama names count as one Meta provider. The list contains every cross-provider flagship pair and every pair within each provider’s newest three.
