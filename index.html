OpenRouter Model Pricing Comparison Tool

Real-Time AI Model Pricing Data

Compare pricing across 400+ AI language models available through OpenRouter. This comprehensive pricing comparison tool helps developers and businesses find the most cost-effective AI models for their specific needs.

Features: Search and filter models by name, capabilities, context length, and cost. Export data in multiple formats. Pricing shown per million tokens for both prompts and completions.

Data is automatically updated every 12 hours to ensure accuracy. Source code available on GitHub.
